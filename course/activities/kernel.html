The calculation

k(x, z) = exp[−γ(x − z)²]. We fit a = (K + αI)⁻¹y, where Kᵢⱼ = k(xᵢ, xⱼ), then predict f(x) = Σᵢ aᵢk(x, xᵢ). There is no fitted intercept or target centring in this example. The fitting objective is Σᵢ(f(xᵢ) − yᵢ)² + αaᵀKa.

These are training observations only. This display supplies no estimate of performance on new observations. For this particular model, the prediction approaches zero far from all three inputs because every kernel contribution approaches zero. That behaviour is a consequence of the construction, not evidence about a chemical system.
